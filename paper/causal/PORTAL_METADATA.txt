Title: recurcast: Coherent Forecasts for History-Dependent Recurrent and Terminal Events in R

Authors: Sunday A. Adetunji, Oluwatimileyin A. Adetunji

Corresponding author: Sunday A. Adetunji, sundayadetunjisa@gmail.com

Abstract:
Event-history software must connect the event definition, observation process and numerical calculation. We present recurcast, an R package with a C++17 core for coherent recurrent and terminal event forecasts. Its observation engine constructs legal paths from tied dates or event-time windows, using canonical enumeration with quadrature or a labelled importance-sampling fallback. Gamma, lognormal and estimated discrete frailty models share likelihood, derivative and filtering interfaces. Sparse ordered uniformization propagates probabilities and rewards without dense transition matrices; a structural fractional-programming solver computes recording-sensitivity bounds on large finite supports. The main API also exposes finite-path MAR and continuous-time right-censoring targeting, with individual influence-function diagnostics and finite-vector asymptotic intervals. Future covariates may be frozen, scheduled or drawn from fitted Gaussian longitudinal submodels. Independent numerical comparisons, scaling measurements, a separately registered targeting experiment and five reproducible examples document the implemented scope. Outputs retain observation assumptions, optimizer status, numerical error and inferential qualifications. A separate baseline binary-treatment interface implements arm-specific g-formula standardization and treatment- and censoring-weighted intensity targeting, with joint influence-function inference for treatment contrasts under explicit causal identification conditions.

Keywords: recurrent events, terminal event, frailty, dynamic prediction, coarsened data, partial identification, R, C++
