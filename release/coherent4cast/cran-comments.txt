## Submission summary

This is a new submission of coherent4cast (version 0.10.2).
Please treat it as the renamed replacement for our pending recurcast 0.10.1
submission, and discontinue the pending review under the earlier name.
The package name was changed to avoid confusion with an existing commercial name.
The earlier package has not been published on CRAN as of this submission.

Package: coherent4cast
Title: Coherent Survival Modelling and Event Forecasting
Maintainer: Sunday A. Adetunji <sundayadetunjisa@gmail.com>
License: GPL-3
Submitted source SHA-256: c0a677f6b434e8fcae47813f878bd335b31923042b659ed5319e9b544c8bb2c0

The package fits recurrent/terminal event-history models and returns common-law
dynamic forecasts, observation-aware sensitivity bounds, targeting diagnostics,
validation measures, and baseline binary-treatment contrasts under explicit
identification assumptions. The rename preserves the R S3/Rcpp/C++17 architecture
and numerical kernels. Legacy fitting/control names remain documented aliases.
